About me

Hi, I’m Behina, a UX/UI designer with a background in Civil Engineering. While working on design projects, I realized that I was most interested in *understanding people’s needs* and creating solutions that make their experiences *easier.*

This discovery led me to UX/UI design, where I can combine creativity, problem-solving, and user-centred thinking. I enjoy researching users’ behaviours and challenges and transforming those insights into simple, intuitive, and visually appealing digital experiences.

I’m a creative, curious, and dependable designer who is always eager to learn, explore new ideas, and create meaningful experiences that support both users and business goals.
